## ⚠ READ FIRST — output discipline

You are producing **ONE PNG FILE.** It is a small UI item icon that
appears in the shop tab and on the equipped agent card. It is NOT a
reference board, item-set strip, or labeled portfolio piece.

Output is a single transparent-background icon at the exact size below.

## SAVE TO

`assets/icons/items/weapon_bit_shard_48.png`

## EXACT OUTPUT DIMENSIONS

**48 × 48 pixels.** Transparent background.

## CONTENT

A weapon icon for the BIT SHARD — the basic-tier attack tool.

- **Subject:** a jagged shard / fragment of glowing cyan crystal. It
  reads as a sharp, breakable, "data fragment" weapon.
- **Composition:** diagonal silhouette running from lower-left to
  upper-right, filling about 80% of the canvas. The shard is asymmetric —
  not a perfect crystal, more like a broken splinter with 2–3 jagged
  facets along each edge.
- **Color:**
  - Inner core: bright cyan `#3df7ff`.
  - Mid-fill: cyan-blue `#62b8ff`.
  - Edge: lighter cyan `#a0fbff`.
  - Outline: `#0a0d18`, 2 px thick.
- **Glow:** a 1-px cyan bloom halo at the upper-right tip of the shard
  (where it would catch the light). Do not let glow extend more than
  2 px outside the silhouette.

## READABILITY TEST

The icon must be readable at **24 px** (half-size). Silhouette should
clearly read as "diagonal jagged shard." If the shape is muddy at small
size, simplify the facets.

## STYLE LOCK

- Pixel art at 48×48 native (not scaled up from a smaller source).
- Hard pixel edges, no painterly soft glow.
- No fine details that won't survive at half-size.

## PALETTE

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Outline | `#0a0d18` |
| Cyan core | `#3df7ff` |
| Cyan mid | `#62b8ff` |
| Cyan edge / highlight | `#a0fbff` |

## DO NOT

- ❌ Output multiple icons or a strip — exactly ONE 48×48 PNG.
- ❌ Add labels, text, weapon names, or callouts.
- ❌ Render the shard floating in a "scene" — it is isolated on
  transparent background.
- ❌ Use colors outside the palette.
- ❌ Add wrist guards, hilts, or anything that makes it look like a
  sword — it is a raw shard fragment.

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENT (repeat)

A single 48 × 48 px PNG with transparent background, showing one cyan
crystal shard. Nothing else on the canvas.
