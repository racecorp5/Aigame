## ⚠ READ FIRST — output discipline

You are producing **ONE PNG FILE.** It is a small UI icon that appears in
the HUD next to the agent's name. It is NOT a reference board, mockup,
multi-icon strip, or labeled portfolio piece.

Output is a single transparent-background icon at the exact size below.

## SAVE TO

`assets/icons/class/compute.png`

## EXACT OUTPUT DIMENSIONS

**48 × 48 pixels.** Transparent background.

## CONTENT

A geometric class icon for the COMPUTE class (burst-damage, surgical
strike). Match the visual vocabulary of the System Breach style board.

- **Shape:** sharp downward-pointing triangle filling roughly 80% of the
  48×48 canvas.
- **Center cutout:** a small chip-die rectangle (8 × 6 px) cut out of the
  triangle's center, suggesting a CPU/microchip.
- **Fill:** cyan `#3df7ff` (the COMPUTE class color).
- **Outline:** `#0a0d18`, 2 px thick.
- **Highlight:** 1-px lighter cyan `#a0fbff` rim along the upper-left edge
  of the triangle (top-left light source).
- **Glow:** very subtle 1–2 px cyan bloom around the outside edge, low
  opacity. Do not let glow extend more than 2 px outside the triangle.

## READABILITY TEST

The icon must be readable at **24 px** (half-size). Test by mentally
shrinking it: silhouette should still clearly read as "downward triangle
with a chip cutout." If silhouette is ambiguous at small size, simplify.

## STYLE LOCK

- Pixel art at 48×48 native (not scaled-up from a smaller source).
- Hard edges, no painterly soft glow.
- Limited to the palette below — no extra colors.

## PALETTE

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Outline | `#0a0d18` |
| Cyan fill | `#3df7ff` |
| Cyan highlight | `#a0fbff` |

## DO NOT

- ❌ Output multiple icons or a strip — exactly ONE 48×48 PNG.
- ❌ Add labels, text, or class names rendered into the art.
- ❌ Use colors outside the palette.
- ❌ Render the icon as part of a UI mockup or HUD strip.

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENT (repeat)

A single 48 × 48 px PNG with transparent background, showing one cyan
class triangle. Nothing else on the canvas.
